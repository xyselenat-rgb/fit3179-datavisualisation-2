Ch 2

Stars follow the sugar

The Health Star Rating scores a product from half a star to five. The sweeter the product, the fewer stars it gets.

🍬
38 gmedian sugar at half a star
What it shows

Half-star products carry a median 38 g of sugar per 100 g

From 3 stars up, the median is just 1.4 g. Correlation between stars and sugar: −0.47.

🍭
What it shows

Some shelves start behind

Snacks & sweets average 1.0 star. Cereals & grains average 3.5, more than three times as many.

🥣
Ch 3

Salt and protein pull too

Sugar is not the only input. Sodium counts against a product, protein counts for it. Both signals are weaker than sugar.

🧂
5,070mg sodium per 100 g, the saltiest product

Only 2% of high-salt products reach 4.5 stars or more

In the low-salt band 32% of products reach 4.5 stars or more. Correlation with stars: −0.28.

Two in three high-protein products score 3.5 stars or more

65% of products with 10 g or more protein reach 3.5 stars, against 42% of those under 5 g. Correlation with stars: +0.24.

Ch 4

Read the small print

A long ingredient list is a rough sign of processing. The allergen line tells you what else is in the factory.

🥜
55items on the longest ingredient list
What it shows

Snacks and pantry foods carry the longest ingredient lists

Median list length is 13 items for Snacks & sweets and Pantry & canned, 3 for Dairy.

None of the Frozen & ready or Bakery products in the data list their ingredients.

📝
What it shows

Gluten and milk are the allergens you meet most

Each is named in 45 of the 79 products with an allergen entry.
